This policy explains how MegaByte handles information when you visit our website, use our learning tools, create a browser-based profile, or contact us.

1. Who we are

MegaByte is a learning and project initiative run by Matru (Mega) and Bisal (Byte). For questions about this policy or a privacy request, email onmegabyte@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

Contact requests. If you send the contact form, we receive the name, email address, topic, and message you enter. The form sends this information to our email service so we can receive and reply to your request. The contact endpoint also uses request information, including the network address supplied to it, for basic abuse prevention.

Browser-based profiles and learning progress. If you create a profile or use a learning tool, the site may save your name, email, profile choices, quiz activity, and progress in local storage or IndexedDB in your browser. These records are kept on that browser/device and are not synced to a MegaByte account server. The profile password is not sent to MegaByte; the browser stores a salted password verifier for local sign-in.

Resume Builder. Information you enter into the resume tool is saved in your browser for its autosave feature. It is not sent to MegaByte by that feature. Avoid entering information you do not want stored on the device or included in an export you create.

Technical information. Our hosting and delivery providers may process standard request and diagnostic information, such as IP address, browser/device details, requested pages, and timestamps, to operate and secure the site. MegaByte does not currently use advertising trackers or a third-party analytics script on the main landing page.

3. How we use information

  • To answer questions, bug reports, feedback, and collaboration requests.
  • To send a service email confirming that we received a contact request, when email delivery is available.
  • To save profile preferences and learning progress locally so the tools work as expected.
  • To secure, maintain, and improve the website and respond to misuse or technical issues.

We do not sell personal information or use contact messages to send marketing newsletters.

4. Sharing and service providers

We share information only as needed to operate the features described here, respond to you, protect the service, or meet a legal obligation.

  • Resend processes contact emails and delivery of acknowledgements. See Resend’s Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel provides website hosting and may process technical request information. See Vercel’s Privacy Notice.
  • Other academies, external services, or project links may have their own operators and privacy practices. Review their policies before sharing information with them.

5. Browser storage and retention

Local profiles and learning records remain in your browser until you remove them, clear the site’s browser data, or the browser removes them. Signing out ends the local signed-in session but does not by itself delete the saved profile or progress.

Contact requests are delivered to the MegaByte inbox and retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, handle follow-up, maintain business records, or meet legal obligations. Email providers may retain delivery and message data under their own policies.

Browser-based storage is not a cloud backup. Clearing browser data, changing devices, or using a private browsing window can remove or make your progress unavailable. Anyone with access to your browser profile or device may be able to access locally saved information.

6. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to submit the contact form and can use the public learning pages without creating a profile. You can clear MegaByte site data in your browser settings to remove locally saved profile and learning information; some tools also provide their own reset controls.

For a copy, correction, or deletion request concerning a contact message held by MegaByte, email us from the address involved and describe your request. We may need to verify it is yours and may retain limited records where required or reasonably necessary. We will respond in line with applicable law.

7. Children and safety

Our learning resources may be useful to students, but the website is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before creating a profile or submitting personal details. A parent or guardian may contact us if they believe a child has sent us personal information.

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, or other sensitive information in the contact form.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the site, its providers, or applicable requirements change. The current version and its update date will appear on this page.

Questions or privacy requests: onmegabyte@gmail.com.
